# Enterprise Direction

Axezent AI PRK-Fuzzer is open source at the finite receipt-generation layer.

Enterprise offerings can be built around:

- signed generated datasets
- private benchmark suites
- hosted receipt-validation dashboards
- custom reduction packs
- classroom and training infrastructure
- high-throughput fuzzing pipelines
- long-term proof-artifact storage

Enterprise truth boundary:

Paid systems generate and validate finite receipts under declared deterministic reduction rules. They do not certify global complexity-theory breakthroughs.
